[Verse — slow, cold, controlled fury]
This time I encounter you, I shall make my sentiments known:
confound the whole lot of you.
Confound your pursuits, confound the hardships you claim,
confound whatever destination you imagine.

[Build — accelerating, piling on]
Confound your circle, confound your faction,
confound your travels, confound your counterfeit glower,
confound your dreary employment, sir!
Confound your airs, confound your shadows,
confound your preening vanity!

[Outro — slowing, final tirade at the assembled]
And as for the hussies assembled here —
should they prove unwilling, confound them as well.
"Who, precisely, are you calling a hussy, you insufferable wretch?"
"All the hussies here, madam."
